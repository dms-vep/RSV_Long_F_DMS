# IgG vs Fab neutralization
In [Simonich et al (2025)](), we explain why mutations to RSV F can have different impacts on IgG versus Fab neutralization depending on the affinity of the Fab for the F variant (these affinities differ for nirsevimab between subtype A and B RSV).

A quantitative biophysical model that explains these differences is [available here](https://jbloomlab.github.io/IgG-vs-Fab-neutralization/notebook.html), and below is an interactive plot that allows you to explore these effects interactively:
